## 🤝 How to Add Yourself as a Contributor

When you open a Pull Request that introduces code, documentation, or design improvements:
1. Fork the repository and create your feature branch.
2. In your PR branch, add your name, GitHub handle, and a brief description of your contribution to the table above.
3. Open your Pull Request.
4. Once your PR is reviewed and merged by the maintainer, your contribution will officially be immortalized here in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`!

For full step-by-step guidance on how to fork, test, and submit your changes, please read our [Contribution Guidelines (CONTRIBUTING.md)](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Thank you for contributing to open-source software! 💜
